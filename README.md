## About

I lead IT and infrastructure at **Simdaasai**. Over five years I have moved from network operations tooling at Bharti Airtel to product engineering at **Cyber3ra** and **SkyAI Technologies** (both at IIT Kanpur), shipping microservices-based products in media, cybersecurity, marketplaces and aerospace.

- I design event-driven backends on Kafka and RabbitMQ and deploy them on Kubernetes with Terraform and CI/CD.
- I care about code quality and observability: Jenkins, SonarQube, Prometheus, Grafana, Sentry.
- I photograph in my free time. See the [portfolio](https://YOUR-PORTFOLIO-URL).
